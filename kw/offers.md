# Òf

Òf sé kat ki ka fè pwomosyon pou on bagay: on manjé èspésyal, on aktivité, on upgrade, oben on pwogwam pou on sézon (pa égzanp simenn Nwèl).

<figure><img src="assets/img/offer-edit.webp" alt="Bwat on òf"><figcaption>On òf ki ka chanjé.</figcaption></figure>

| Bwat | Sa i ka fè |
|---|---|
| **Offer Type** | Dining, Experience, Upgrade, Generic oben Seasonal Update |
| **Card Title** / **Note** | Tèks-la asou kat-la. Si tit-la vid, app-la ka sèvi tit bagay-la |
| **Placement Screen** | Koté i ka paèt: Today, Dining oben Experiences |
| **Active From / Active To** | Jou yo kat-la ka paèt |
| **Linked Excursion / Linked Daily Content** | Si ou vlé. ID aktivité-a oben manjé-a kat-la ka ouvè |
| **CTA Label / CTA Action** | Tèks bouton-an, épi sa i ka fè: anyen (View Only), voyé on mésaj ba desk-la, oben ouvè on lyen |
| **Seasonal Intro / Seasonal Programme** | Pou òf sézon: on ti entwodiksyon, épi yon lign pou chak jou. Mété chak lè asou lign pa'y: `7:00pm - 8:00pm | Carols by the pool` |

?> **Ki koté pou jwenn on ID?** Ouvè aktivité-a oben manjé-a. ID-a sé nimewo-a apwé `post=` adan adwès navigatè-a, pa égzanp `post.php?post=409&action=edit`.
