# Resort Settings

One page holds all resort-wide content. Open **Resort Settings**, open the section you need, change it, and press **Save** at the bottom.

<figure><img src="assets/img/resort-settings.webp" alt="Resort Settings with its sections"><figcaption>Resort Settings. Each section opens and closes.</figcaption></figure>

| Section | What it controls in the app |
|---|---|
| **Hosts** | The team cards on the Hosts screen (name, role, photo, short bio) and the “Meet the full team” link |
| **Services** | The services list on the Resort screen, and the Mosquito Guide PDF |
| **Family Foundation** | The Family Foundation text and the Sustainable Travel link |
| **Water Sports Waiver** | The waiver text, the privacy note and the privacy link shown at check-in |
| **Property Map** | The map (PDF or image) and the Guest Directory PDF |
| **Standing Hours** | The Dining intro and the hours of breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, dinner, light bites and the bar |
| **Island & Things to Do** | The Island guide cards in Explore |
| **Front Desk Contact** | The email that receives bookings, messages, check-ins and feedback. **Change it carefully** |
| **Guest Reviews** | The TripAdvisor and Google review links |

## Island guide cards

Each card has a **Title**, an **Image** and a **Description**. The order of the rows is the order in the app; drag a row to move it. Every card ends with a *Plan this with us* button that sends a message to the desk.

<figure><img src="assets/img/resort-island.webp" alt="The Island and Things to Do rows in Resort Settings"><figcaption>Island cards, one row each.</figcaption></figure>

The description accepts simple formatting:

| You type | Guests see |
|---|---|
| An empty line between two texts | A new paragraph |
| `Pigeon Island` | **Pigeon Island** (bold) |
| `- Sunset cruise` at the start of a line | • Sunset cruise (a bullet point) |

!> **No undo here** Resort Settings has no drafts and no history. Before a big change, copy the old text into a document, just in case.
