# Stays

A **Stay** is one guest's visit: who they are, which room, and the dates. Each stay has its own **private link**, which is how the guest gets into the app.

## Create a stay

1. Go to **Stays → Add New Stay**.
2. Fill in **Stay Details** (see the table below). You can leave the title empty: WordPress writes it for you as *Guest name – Room type – Room number*.
3. Press **Publish**. The private link is created at that moment.

<figure><img src="assets/img/stay-edit.webp" alt="The Stay Details fields of a stay"><figcaption>The Stay Details box of our example guest, Judith Tester.</figcaption></figure>

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Guest Full Name** | As the guest wants to be greeted. The app says “Good morning, …” |
| **Guest Email** | The guest's email. Used to recognise the guest and to show their bookings |
| **Room** | The number shown in **Rooms** next to the room (its ID), not the room number on the door. See [Rooms](/en/rooms) |
| **Adults / Children** | Party size |
| **Additional rooms** | Only for families in more than one room: the other room numbers, separated by commas (for example `206, 208`) |
| **Check-in Date / Check-out Date** | The most important fields. They decide what the guest sees (see [guest states](/en/?id=how-the-guest-app-works)) |
| **Status** | For your own reference. The app does *not* use it: the dates decide |
| Traveling With Agent, Family Foundation Opt-In, Water Sports Waiver Accepted | Filled in by the guest at check-in. You can correct them |
| Housekeeping Frequency, Extra Pillows, Restock Minibar Wine, Special Requests | Housekeeping preferences. The guest changes them in **My Stay**, and you see their answers here. If you change them here, the guest sees your change. They are the same data |
| Upgrade Interest Note | Filled in when a guest shows interest in an upgrade |

## Send the private link

1. Open the stay. Under the title you will see the **Permalink**, ending in `/my-stay/…/`. That is the private link.
2. Copy it, or press **View Stay** at the top and copy the address from the browser.
3. Send it to the guest only, by email or as a QR code at check-in.

<figure><img src="assets/img/stay-link.webp" alt="The permalink of a stay, which is the guest's private link"><figcaption>(1) The permalink is the private link. (2) <strong>View Stay</strong> opens the app as the guest sees it.</figcaption></figure>

!> **Do not edit the permalink** The end of the link is a secret code. If you change it, the link the guest already has stops working.

?> **Recommended browsers** Guests get the best result with **Safari on iPhone** and **Chrome on Android**. After check-in, the app offers a *Get East Winds on your device* button to add it to the home screen.

## Change the dates of a stay

1. Open the stay and change **Check-in Date** and/or **Check-out Date**.
2. Press **Update**. The guest's link keeps working; only what they see changes.

Example: Judith's stay says *Check-in 13 October*. If she arrives a day late, change it to 14 October and press Update. Until then her app shows the pre-arrival welcome; on the 14th it opens completely. Set the check-out to yesterday and she is no longer on property.

## When a guest leaves

You don't need to do anything: after the check-out date, booking closes by itself. Keep the stay for your records. If a stay was created by mistake, move it to the Trash. Its link stops working.
