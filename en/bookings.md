# Bookings

When a guest presses **Request to book** in the app, a new booking appears in **Exc. Bookings**, and the front desk receives an email. Your job is to check it and answer: *Approve* or *Decline*.

## The booking queue

<figure><img src="assets/img/bookings-list.webp" alt="The Exc. Bookings list with statuses and the Approve and Decline links"><figcaption>Exc. Bookings. (1) The Spa view. (2) Hover over a row to see <strong>Approve</strong> and <strong>Decline</strong>. (3) The status, with the decline reason under it.</figcaption></figure>

- Each row shows the guest and experience (title), the **Excursion**, the number of **Guests** and the **Status**.
- The number in a circle next to **Exc. Bookings** in the menu counts requests waiting for an answer. It goes down as soon as you answer one.
- Above the list, the **Spa** view shows only spa treatment requests, for the spa team.

| Status | Meaning | The guest sees |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="pill pending">pending</span> | New request, waiting for you. It already holds the spots | **Requested** · “We'll confirm with you shortly.” |
| <span class="pill confirmed">confirmed</span> | You approved it | **Confirmed** · “See you there!” |
| <span class="pill declined">declined</span> | You declined it. The spots are free again | **Not available**, with the reason you chose |

## Approve a booking

1. Open **Exc. Bookings**.
2. Move the mouse over the booking (on a tablet, tap the row) and press **Approve**, in green.
3. The status changes to <span class="pill confirmed">confirmed</span>. The guest sees *Confirmed* the next time they open the app.

## Decline a booking

1. Press **Decline**, in red, under the booking.
2. A window opens. Choose a **reason**. It is required, so the guest always knows why.
3. If you choose **Other**, write a short note. The guest reads it, so write it for them.
4. Press the decline button in the window. The status changes to <span class="pill declined">declined</span>.

<figure><img src="assets/img/booking-decline.webp" alt="The decline window with the list of reasons"><figcaption>The decline window. You cannot decline without a reason.</figcaption></figure>

| Reason | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Bad weather | Rain, wind or sea conditions cancel the activity |
| Event Re-scheduling | The activity moves to another day or time |
| Insufficient Quorum | Not enough guests signed up for it to run |
| Exceeded Quorum | It is full |
| Other | Anything else. A note is required |

<figure class="phone"><img src="assets/img/app-my-bookings.webp" alt="My Bookings in the guest app showing confirmed and declined bookings with a reason"><figcaption>The guest sees the result in <strong>Book → My Bookings</strong>.</figcaption></figure>

## Change your answer

You can change a decision at any time from the same row:

- A <span class="pill declined">declined</span> booking shows **Approve**. Approving it removes the decline reason.
- A <span class="pill confirmed">confirmed</span> booking shows **Decline**, for example when the weather changes. The reason window opens as usual.

!> **The app does not send an email to the guest** The guest only sees the new status inside the app. If you decline something close to its start time, or change a confirmed booking, also call the room or tell the guest in person.

## Booking details

Click the booking title to open it. The **Booking details** box shows the experience, the date and time, the number of guests, the guest's name and email, their stay, and when they asked. The **Approve** and **Decline** buttons are here too.

<figure><img src="assets/img/booking-detail.webp" alt="The Booking details box of one booking"><figcaption>One booking, open. Click the experience or the stay to jump to them.</figcaption></figure>

## Spa requests

Spa treatments work a little differently:

- A guest can request the **same treatment more than once** during a stay, for up to **4 people**.
- Spa treatments have no fixed time. **Before approving, the spa team contacts the guest to agree the time.** Then approve.
- Use the **Spa** view at the top of Exc. Bookings to see only spa requests.
