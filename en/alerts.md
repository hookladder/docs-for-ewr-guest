# Alerts

An alert is a short notice shown to guests on the **Today** screen, for example a closed pool, a change of venue for dinner, or a weather warning.

<figure class="phone"><img src="assets/img/app-alert.webp" alt="An alert on the Today screen of the guest app"><figcaption>An alert as guests see it.</figcaption></figure>

## Post an alert

1. Go to **Alerts → Add New Alert**.
2. Write a short **title**, for example “Beach bar closes early today”. The title is for the team: guests do not see it.
3. In **Message**, write one or two sentences: what happens, when, and what the guest can do instead. This is the text guests see, under *Property notice*.
4. Choose the **Urgency**: *Urgent* for safety and things that affect everyone right now, *Routine* for everything else.
5. Set **Starts At** and **Ends At**. The alert only shows between those two moments.
6. Turn **Active** on, and press **Publish**.

<figure><img src="assets/img/alert-edit.webp" alt="The fields of an alert"><figcaption>An alert being written.</figcaption></figure>

?> **Stop an alert early** Turn **Active** off and press Update. Keep the alert: next time you only need to change the dates and switch it on again.
