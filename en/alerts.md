# Alerts

An alert is a short notice shown to guests on the **Today** screen, for example a closed pool, a change of venue for dinner, a weather warning or live music tonight. Each alert has a **Type**, which sets its colour, icon and heading.

## Post an alert

1. Go to **Alerts → Add New Alert**.
2. Write a short **title**, for example “Beach bar closes early today”. The title is for the team: guests do not see it.
3. In **Message**, write one or two sentences: what happens, when, and what the guest can do instead. This is the text guests see, under the heading of its type.
4. Choose the **Type** (see the table below).
5. Set **Starts At** and **Ends At**. The alert only shows between those two moments.
6. Turn **Active** on, and press **Publish**.

## Alert types

| Type | Guests see | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Coral card, **!** icon, heading *Urgent notice* | Safety, or a closure that affects everyone right now |
| **Information** | Blue card, **i** icon, heading *Good to know* | Changes of time or place, practical notices |
| **Weather** | Teal card, cloud icon, heading *Weather* | Rain, wind or sea conditions, and activities paused because of them |
| **Event or celebration** | Gold card, star icon, heading *Today at East Winds* | Something special today: live music, a party, a themed dinner |

The app shows up to **three** alerts at a time: urgent ones first, then weather, information and events. Keep alerts few and short, so the urgent ones stand out.

?> **Stop an alert early** Turn **Active** off and press Update. Keep the alert: next time you only need to change the dates and switch it on again.
