# Rooms

Each room is one item with its **Room Number**, **Room Type** (Superior, Deluxe, Suite, Villa…), floor, capacity, amenities and gallery. Stays point to a room, and the room type and number appear in the stay title and in the app.

<figure><img src="assets/img/room-edit.webp" alt="The Room Details fields"><figcaption>Room Details.</figcaption></figure>

?> **Room ID vs room number** The **Room** field of a stay needs the room's *ID*: the number after `post=` in the address when the room is open. It is not the number on the door.
