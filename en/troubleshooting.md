# Problems & solutions

The most common situations, and what to do.

## Guests

### A guest opens their link and sees “no stay”, or a general welcome

- Check that the stay exists, is published, and is not in the Trash.
- Check that the link is complete. It must end in `/my-stay/` plus the code. Copy it again from the stay.
- Ask the guest to open it in **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android), not inside another app (Instagram, WhatsApp preview…).
- If the guest is on a new network and still sees nothing, the server's security check may have blocked the first visit. Ask them to open the link a second time. If it keeps happening, write to support.

### A guest says they cannot book anything

- **Before the check-in date**, booking is closed (“Booking opens when you check in”). Check the stay dates.
- **The check-in form** must be completed first. It appears the first time the guest opens the app on property.
- The experience may be **Cancelled**, or have no spots left. Check its Status and Spots Available.
- Some items say “No booking needed. Just come along.” That is correct: they are open to everyone.

### A guest added the app to the iPhone home screen and now it asks for the link again

On iPhone, the home screen app does not share Safari's memory. Ask the guest to delete the icon, open their private link again in **Safari**, and add it again with the **Get East Winds on your device** button in My Stay (it gives the right steps).

### The guest still sees old information after I changed it

- Check that you pressed **Update** (or **Save**) and that a confirmation message appeared.
- Menus, alerts and offers have no Preview button: check them in the app itself. Menu titles never show in the app.
- Ask the guest to pull down to refresh, or to close and reopen the app.
- Check the dates: menus need the right **Content Date**, experiences the right days, offers and alerts the right start and end.

### An activity does not appear under the “Today” or “This week” filter

Items *By request* (spa, water sports, the pool) have no date, so they only appear under **All upcoming**. This is a known limitation and will improve.

## Bookings

### The app says “Fully booked” but almost nobody is going

Today, spots are counted across *all* bookings ever made for that item, not only for the next date, so weekly activities slowly fill up. Until this is fixed, raise **Spots Available** and press Save. This is a known issue and support is working on it.

### I pressed Decline but nothing changed, or I saw a red error

A decline needs a reason, and **Other** also needs a note. Try again and fill both in.

### I approved or declined the wrong booking

Change it from the same row: **Approve** on a declined booking, or **Decline** on a confirmed one. Then let the guest know.

### The number next to Exc. Bookings does not go down

It counts bookings still <span class="pill pending">pending</span>. Answer them, or reload the page. Bookings in the Trash are not counted.

## Admin

### I deleted something by mistake

1. Open the list where it was (for example Excursions).
2. Click **Trash** above the list.
3. Hover over the item and press **Restore**.

Items stay in the Trash for 30 days. Items deleted from Media, or with *Delete Permanently*, cannot be restored. Write to support.

### “Sorry, you are not allowed to access this page”

Your role does not include that screen. That is on purpose: it protects the parts of the site that only developers should change. If you think you need it, write to support.

### Someone else is editing this item

WordPress warns you when another person has the same item open. Agree on who continues: if two people save, the last one wins.

### A date or time looks wrong by a few hours

All dates are in Saint Lucia time. If you are working from another country, the times are still Saint Lucia's.

### The site is slow, or shows an error page

Wait one minute and reload. If it happens again, write to support with the time and what you were doing. A screenshot helps a lot.
