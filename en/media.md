# Pictures & files

All pictures and PDFs live in **Media**. You can upload new ones there or directly from any edit screen.

<figure><img src="assets/img/media-library.webp" alt="The Media library"><figcaption>The Media library.</figcaption></figure>

## Change a picture

1. Open the experience.
2. Click the current picture at the top of the right panel (or **Set featured image** if there is none). A window opens.
3. Open the **Media Library** tab and click a picture, or use **Upload files** to add a new one from your computer.
4. On the right, fill in **Alt Text**: a few words saying what the picture shows. It helps guests who use a screen reader.
5. Press **Set featured image**, then **Save**.

<figure><img src="assets/img/media-featured.webp" alt="The window to choose a featured image"><figcaption>Choosing a featured image. Never press <em>Delete permanently</em> here.</figcaption></figure>

| Good pictures | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Landscape (wider than tall), at least 1600 px wide | Very small pictures: they look blurry on phones |
| JPG or WebP, under 1 MB | Photos straight from a camera (10 MB or more): the app gets slow |
| The subject in the centre: cards cut the edges | Text inside the picture: it gets cut and can't be translated |

!> **Don't delete pictures from Media** A picture can be used in several places. Deleting it from the library leaves empty spaces in the app. To replace a picture, set a new one as described above.
