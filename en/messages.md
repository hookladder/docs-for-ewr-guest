# Desk messages

Guests can write to the resort from the app: *Message the desk*, *Say hello* to a host, and *Plan this with us* on the Island guide. Each message is saved in **Desk Messages** and also emailed to the front desk.

<figure><img src="assets/img/messages-list.webp" alt="The Desk Messages list with read and unread messages"><figcaption>Desk Messages. The <strong>Status</strong> column shows who read each message, and when.</figcaption></figure>

1. Open **Desk Messages**. The number next to it counts unread messages.
2. Click a message to read it. Opening it marks it as read, with your name and the time.
3. Answer the guest by email (the address is in the message), by phone, or in person. The app has no reply button yet.

<figure><img src="assets/img/message-detail.webp" alt="One desk message, open"><figcaption>One message, open.</figcaption></figure>

?> **Tip** Messages from the Island guide start with “I'd like to plan: …” and the name of the card, so you know what the guest is interested in.
