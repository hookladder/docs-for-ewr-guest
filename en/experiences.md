# Experiences & events

Everything a guest can do or book is an item in **Excursions**: tours, classes, cocktails, water sports, and every spa treatment. The guest sees them in **Explore → Experiences**, on **Today**, and in **Book**.

## Change an experience

Excursions open in the newer WordPress editor, which looks different from the other screens. Open the item from the list, change what you need, and press **Save** (top right).

<figure><img src="assets/img/excursion-edit-main.webp" alt="The excursion editor: featured image, excerpt, categories, Save button and the Meta Boxes bar"><figcaption>(2) Featured image. (3) Excerpt. (4) Categories. (5) Save. (6) The <strong>Meta Boxes</strong> bar opens the Excursion Details.</figcaption></figure>

| Part | Where | Shown in the app as |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Big text at the top | The name on the card and on the detail screen |
| **Description** | Under the title (“Type / to choose a block”). Click and type; Enter starts a new paragraph | The full text on the detail screen |
| **Featured image** | Right panel, at the top | The picture on the card and at the top of the detail screen. See [Change a picture](/en/media?id=change-a-picture) |
| **Excerpt** | Right panel, *Add an excerpt…* | The short line on the card. Keep it to one sentence |
| **Excursion Categories** | Right panel, at the bottom (click to open) | The filters in Explore. Spa treatments must be in one of the *Spa & Wellness* sub-categories |
| **Excursion Details** | Bottom of the screen: click the **Meta Boxes** bar, or drag it up | Schedule, price, meeting point, status, spots. See the next table |

?> **Can't see the right panel?** Click the square icon next to **Save** to show or hide it. If a “Welcome to the editor” window appears, close it with the ×.

<figure><img src="assets/img/excursion-edit-details.webp" alt="The Excursion Details box"><figcaption>The Excursion Details box.</figcaption></figure>

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Excursion Type** | *Complimentary* (included) or *Paid* |
| **Price** / **Price Note** | Only for paid items. The note is for prices that are not one number, for example “$75 per child”. Guests pay at the resort |
| **Duration** | Free text, for example “3 hrs” |
| **Recurrence** | How often it happens. The most important field, explained below |
| **Recurrence Days** | Weekly items only: the days as numbers, separated by commas. 1 = Monday … 7 = Sunday. Example: `3,5` = Wednesday and Friday. After saving it may show as `,3,5,`: that is normal |
| **Schedule Date** | One-off items only. For weekly items the app fills it in by itself every night |
| **Schedule Time / End Time** | Start time, and optional end time (shown as “8:00am - 9:00am”) |
| **Schedule Note** | Optional, for example “Summer: 4:30pm - 7:30pm” |
| **Meet Point** / **What to Bring** | Practical information for the guest |
| **Status** | *Available*, *Cancelled* or *Rescheduled*. See below |
| **Featured** | Highlights the item in the app |
| **Spots Available** | How many guests can book it. Pending and confirmed requests take spots; declined ones free them. Leave it empty only for *By request* items (no limit); on other items an empty value tells guests to ask the front desk |

## The three kinds of schedule

| Recurrence | Use it for | How to fill it |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Things on the weekly programme (yoga on Wednesdays and Fridays) | Set **Recurrence Days** and **Schedule Time**. The app always shows the next date |
| **One-off** | A single event (a special dinner, a holiday party) | Set **Schedule Date** and **Schedule Time**. After that day it disappears from the app |
| **By request** | Things with no fixed date: water sports, spa, the pool | No date needed. Guests request it and you agree the time with them |

## Cancel or move an experience

- **Cancel:** set **Status** to *Cancelled* and press Update. Guests see “This experience has been cancelled” and cannot book it. Then decline its pending bookings with the right reason (see [Bookings](/en/bookings)).
- **Move:** change the date, days or time, set **Status** to *Rescheduled*, and press Update. Tell the guests who already booked.
- **Bring it back:** set **Status** to *Available* again.

## Add a new experience

1. Go to **Excursions → Add New Excursion**.
2. Write the title, the description and a one-line excerpt.
3. Choose the categories and set a featured image.
4. Fill in **Excursion Details**: type, recurrence, days or date, time, meet point, spots.
5. Press **Publish** (top right, then confirm) and check it in the app.

?> **Easier: start from a similar item** Before creating something new, check whether an old one can be updated with new dates. This keeps the list short (golden rule 2).
