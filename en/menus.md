# Menus

Each meal of each day is one item in **Daily Content**. The app shows today's menus on **Today** (the meal of the moment comes first) and in **Explore → Dining**.

?> **One menu per meal, per day** The app shows one breakfast, one lunch, one Afternoon Tea and one dinner for each day. If two menus share the same date and meal, only one of them appears, so give every menu its own **Meal**.

<figure><img src="assets/img/menus-list.webp" alt="The Daily Content list with one item per meal and day"><figcaption>Daily Content: one item per meal and day.</figcaption></figure>

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The restaurant and meal, for example “Bird of Paradise Restaurant — Lunch”. It helps the team find the menu in the list; guests do not see it |
| **Content Date** | The day this menu is served |
| **Meal** | Breakfast, Lunch, Afternoon Tea or Dinner. Afternoon Tea shows in the app from lunchtime until 5pm |
| **Menu Items** | One row per dish. Use **+ Add item** for a new row, and drag rows to change the order |
| **Wine Pairing** / **Cocktail of the Day** | Optional |
| **Featured** | Highlights this menu on Today and Dining |

## Prepare next week's menus

The resort alternates two sets of menus. Instead of writing new ones:

1. Open a menu from a day that has passed.
2. Change **Content Date** to the new day. Change dishes only if needed.
3. Press **Update**. Repeat for the other meals.

?> **Meal times** The hours shown in the app are not in the menus. They are in [Resort Settings → Standing Hours](/en/resort-settings), including the Afternoon Tea time.

?> **Checking a change** Menus have no page of their own, so there is no Preview button. After you press **Update**, open **Today** or **Explore → Dining** in the app to see the result.
