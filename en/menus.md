# Menus

Each meal of each day is one item in **Daily Content**. The app shows today's menus on **Today** (the meal of the moment comes first) and in **Explore → Dining**.

?> **One menu per meal, per day** The app shows one breakfast, one lunch, one Island Bites, one Afternoon Tea and one dinner for each day. If two menus share the same date and meal, only one of them appears, so give every menu its own **Meal**.

<figure><img src="assets/img/menus-list.webp" alt="The Daily Content list with one item per meal and day"><figcaption>Daily Content: one item per meal and day.</figcaption></figure>

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The restaurant and meal, for example “Bird of Paradise Restaurant — Lunch”. It helps the team find the menu in the list; guests do not see it |
| **Content Date** | The day this menu is served |
| **Meal** | Breakfast, Lunch, Island Bites, Afternoon Tea or Dinner. Island Bites shows next to lunch from 11:30am to 5pm (see [Island Bites](/en/menus?id=island-bites)); Afternoon Tea shows from lunchtime until 5pm |
| **Menu Items** | One row per section of the menu (for example *Appetizers*, *Main Course*). Put the section name in **Dish Name** and its dishes in **Description**, one dish per line (press Enter between dishes). Use **+ Add item** for a new row, and drag rows to change the order |
| **Wine Pairing** / **Cocktail of the Day** | Optional |
| **Featured** | Highlights this menu on Today and Dining |
| **Featured image** (right column) | The photo at the top of the menu card on Today and of the menu page. Use a landscape photo of the restaurant or the dishes. Without one, the card shows no photo |

## Writing a menu by sections

Most menus have several choices per course. Write one row per course:

| Dish Name | Description |
|---|---|
| Appetizers | Vietnamese Spring Rolls with Sweet Chili Sauce (sf)<br>Duck Breast Salad with Walnut Dressing (n)(df)(gf)<br>Fish Skewers with Capers and Cream Sauce (gf) |

The app shows the section name as a heading and every line of the description as a dish with its own bullet, so you do not need to type bullets yourself. A description with a blank line, or a single sentence, is shown as a note instead, as typed: use this for the menu key or allergy advice.

On **Today**, each menu card shows a short taste of the menu: the first dish of each section, up to three. Put the dish you want guests to notice first at the top of each section.

?> **Tip** Copying a menu from a Word or PDF document can join the lines together. If dishes appear on one line in the app, open the menu and press Enter between them.

## Island Bites

**Island Bites** is the casual menu served from 11:30am to 5pm, with lunch and at the Sunset Bar. It has its own **Meal**, so it never takes the place of the lunch menu.

1. Open the Island Bites menu in **Daily Content** (or create it with **Add New**).
2. In **Meal**, choose **Island Bites**, not *Lunch*.
3. Like every menu, it appears on the day in **Content Date**. Move its date to the new day together with the other menus, then press **Update**.

The app shows it as its own card next to lunch on **Today**, and as its own tab on the menu page, with **11:30am – 5pm** next to the title. These hours are fixed in the app: you do not type them anywhere.

!> **Not under Lunch** If Island Bites is saved as *Lunch*, it competes with the lunch menu for the same place and one of the two disappears from the app.

?> **The introduction** Write the short explanation at the top (“This casual menu is available…”) as a note: one sentence, or text with a blank line in it. Otherwise the app shows each line as a dish.

## Prepare next week's menus

The resort alternates two sets of menus. Instead of writing new ones:

1. Open a menu from a day that has passed.
2. Change **Content Date** to the new day. Change dishes only if needed.
3. Press **Update**. Repeat for the other meals.

?> **Meal times** The hours shown in the app are not in the menus. They are in [Resort Settings → Standing Hours](/en/resort-settings), including the Afternoon Tea time. Island Bites always shows 11:30am – 5pm.

?> **Checking a change** Menus have no page of their own, so there is no Preview button. After you press **Update**, open **Today** or **Explore → Dining** in the app to see the result.
